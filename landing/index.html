AudioShape

Free, open source music player with live audio effect editing such as EQ and speed control. Also includes a built-in YouTube/Spotify downloader.

Download for Windows Try in browser →

Features

01 Live Audio Editing Adjust EQ, reverb, speed, and other effects in real time while music plays. Hear changes instantly without stopping playback.
02 Persistent Per-Track Settings Effects are saved to each track. Switch songs and your settings come back automatically — no reapplying every time.
03 Export & Presets Bake your effects into an exported file, or save effect chains as named presets to reuse across your library.
04 YouTube & Spotify Downloader Download audio from YouTube or Spotify directly inside the app. No separate tools, no command line.
05 Fullscreen Video Mode Supports video formats with a dedicated fullscreen playback view — doubles as a lightweight media player.
06 Custom Themes Swap color schemes to match your setup. The interface adapts without restarting the app.
07 Open Source AudioShape is completely free and open source.

Why try AudioShape

Persistent effect settings

Set EQ, speed, and reverb once per track. AudioShape remembers your exact settings automatically.

Desktop-first workflow

Browse your local library, edit in real time, and export files without jumping between multiple tools.

Actively maintained

New improvements and bug fixes are shipped regularly in open source.

Yes. It is free, open source, and available on GitHub.

What OS is supported?

AudioShape currently targets Windows.

Can I use AudioShape in a browser?

Yes. You can also try the browser version at slowandreverbify.com.
